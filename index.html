Vektograf is a forward-thinking graphic design studio built on the belief that perception is the ultimate canvas. We work at the intersection of visual identity, editorial design, and art direction — pushing colour, form, and concept until something genuinely new appears.

Founded on the idea that design should provoke as much as it communicates, we partner with brands, artists, and institutions who want work that refuses to disappear into the background.
